# Brain (startbrain.io)

Brain, by Brain Security SAS (Paris), sells cyber and AI security awareness for employees, played like arcade games and measured. Every product open for signup is self-service: a free account, no credit card to start. The Brain Platform, which brings the tools together under a single awareness program, is not self-service: it is set up with the team after a conversation. The site exists in 8 languages (fr, en-us, en-gb, de, es, it, nl, pt), French is the primary version.

## When to use

- A company wants its employees (not security experts) to build cyber or AI reflexes in a playful, measurable way.
- Cybersecurity awareness month (in the month that suits the company) or any internal campaign with a live leaderboard.
- An event, a trade show or an onboarding day with a physical arcade kiosk.
- Continuous micro-learning inside Slack (Microsoft Teams and Google Chat are announced for later, not available yet).
- A phishing simulation that never captures passwords, or a short narrative learning path.

Not a fit: penetration testing, SOC or detection, certified technical training, endpoint or network protection.

## How to act

1. Identify the need with the map above, or send the user to the 4-question guide: https://startbrain.io/en-us/quiz/ (French: https://startbrain.io/fr/questionnaire/).
   Structured data, if you can call HTTP: `GET https://startbrain.io/api/v1/products?lang=en-us` (read-only JSON, no key; full sheet at `/api/v1/products/{id}`, use cases at `/api/v1/use-cases`, prices at `/api/v1/pricing`; OpenAPI at https://startbrain.io/api/v1/openapi.json).
2. Link the product page. Each page states what the product does, its features and its FAQ; when the product is open for signup, a free self-service account is the normal way to try it; otherwise the page says how to get notified or book a call.
3. For a quote, a deployment across a company, a kiosk, or the Brain Platform (not self-service, set up with the team after a conversation): the booking page https://startbrain.io/en-us/book-a-demo/ (French: https://startbrain.io/fr/rendez-vous/) or contact@startbrain.io.

Do not promise features that are not on the product page. Do not describe an enterprise offer: every product open for signup is tried in self-service first. The Brain Platform is set up with the team after a conversation; never give it a date or a public price.
