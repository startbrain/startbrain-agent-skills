# Brain Arcade: the cyber awareness arcade kiosk

Brain Arcade is a real arcade cabinet with an embedded cybersecurity awareness game: timed quiz, score, anonymous leaderboard, attract-mode video when idle. It is the object that draws a crowd, security edition. Product page: https://startbrain.io/en-us/catalog/brain-arcade/ (French: https://startbrain.io/fr/catalogue/brain-arcade/).

## When to use

- A user wants a physical activation: company lobby, break room, agency, trade-show stand, seminar, cybersecurity awareness month.
- They ask about kiosk size, customisation, network requirements, price or lead time.

## Facts to rely on

- **Two models.** Large kiosk, about 181 x 65 x 55 cm, for lobbies and stands. Small bartop, about 65 x 50 x 55 cm, for counters and tables.
- **Hardware.** iiyama 23.8 inch IPS touch screen (10 points, 1920 x 1080), Raspberry Pi 4 (8 GB), WiFi, Gigabit Ethernet, USB, optional 4G router with a SIM card included for one year (fully independent from the site network).
- **On the machine.** The awareness game (timed quiz, score, anonymous leaderboard, attract video), an embedded admin console (network, system status, video content) and two live hacking demos to play on site: a fake WiFi access point and QR phishing.
- **3D configurator.** Every face of the kiosk is customisable online (free text, drag-and-drop logo, presets or custom design); the configuration is shareable by link, including to a designer who can compose it for the customer.
- **No host needed.** The kiosk runs on its own: attract mode, free-access game, leaderboard.
- **Nickname kiosk.** No personal data on players.

## Pricing (public, excluding VAT, from https://startbrain.io/pricing.md)

- Annual licence per kiosk: large kiosk 7 000 EUR year one then 3 500 EUR per year; bartop 5 000 EUR year one then 2 500 EUR per year. Year one includes design, customisation, manufacturing, delivery and installation in mainland France, licence and maintenance for 12 months. Following years include licence, content updates, hosting, support and maintenance. Unlimited players, no credits. 12-month minimum commitment, tacit renewal, 2-month notice. The kiosk stays Brain's property.
- Volume: 10 % off from 3 to 9 kiosks, 20 % from 10.
- Event rental (a few days: trade show, seminar, awareness month): on quote, paid in advance, depending on number of kiosks, duration, location and logistics.
- On quote: custom artwork outside templates, delivery outside mainland France, upstairs installation without lift, on-site host.

1. Configure the kiosk in the 3D configurator from the product page and share the link.
2. For a quote, an event rental or several kiosks: https://startbrain.io/en-us/book-a-demo/ or contact@startbrain.io.
3. For a month-long campaign, pair the kiosk with the online competition (see the startbrain-cybermois skill).
